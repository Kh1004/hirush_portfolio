TRAINING
We offer comprehensive training programs tailored for beginners in the field of electrical design. Our courses cover essential topics such as:
Component Selection: We provide in-depth guidance on selecting the right electrical components, including switches, relays, circuit breakers, and more, to ensure optimal system performance and safety.
Protection Calculation: Our training includes practical exercises in protection calculation methods, helping participants understand how to safeguard electrical systems against overloads and faults.
Operating Methods: We educate beginners on the various operating methods and principles that underlie electrical systems, from basic circuits to complex control systems.
Conceptual Understanding: We focus on building a strong foundational understanding of electrical concepts, ensuring that participants grasp the fundamental principles of electrical design.
Our training programs are designed to empower beginners with the knowledge and skills necessary to excel in the field of electrical design, fostering a solid foundation for their future endeavors.

In addition to our foundational training in electrical design, we also offer comprehensive training in PLC programming and HMI/SCADA development. Our programs are structured to take participants from basic concepts to the advanced skills required for potential project-level work. Here's an overview of what our PLC programming and HMI/SCADA development training entails:
PLC Programming Training:
Basic Concepts: We start with an introduction to PLCs, covering their architecture, programming languages, and basic functions.
Programming Languages: Participants learn the fundamentals of ladder logic, function block diagrams, and structured text, gaining proficiency in writing and troubleshooting PLC code.
Advanced Topics: We delve into advanced PLC programming techniques, including PID control, motion control, and data communication between PLCs.
Hands-On Projects: Our training includes hands-on projects to apply the knowledge gained and develop practical skills.
HMI/SCADA Development Training:
Introduction to HMI/SCADA: We provide an overview of Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI) and Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems, explaining their role in industrial automation.
HMI Development: Participants learn to create user-friendly and visually appealing HMI screens for controlling and monitoring industrial processes.
SCADA Configuration: We cover SCADA configuration, data acquisition, trending, and alarm handling to enable participants to build robust SCADA systems.
Real-World Applications: Our training includes real-world case studies and projects, allowing participants to apply their HMI/SCADA development skills.
By offering comprehensive training in PLC programming and HMI/SCADA development, we empower participants to excel in the field of industrial automation, from understanding the basics to tackling complex projects with confidence.


Controller Design 
We specialize in designing customized electronics-based controllers tailored to meet specific customer requirements. Our expertise encompasses a wide range of controller types, including IoT controllers and monitoring controllers. Here's an overview of our capabilities in this area:
1. IoT Controllers:
Needs Assessment: We work closely with customers to understand their IoT control system requirements, including data collection, remote monitoring, and control objectives.
Hardware Design: Our experienced engineers design hardware platforms that are optimized for IoT applications, incorporating connectivity options such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and LoRa, depending on the project's needs.
Firmware Development: We develop custom firmware to enable seamless communication between sensors, actuators, and the IoT platform. Security and data encryption are prioritized.
Integration: We integrate the IoT controller with cloud platforms, enabling real-time data access and control through web and mobile applications.
Testing and Validation: Rigorous testing and validation ensure the controller's reliability and performance under various conditions.
Scalability: Our designs are scalable, allowing for future expansion and adaptation to changing IoT requirements.
2. Monitoring Controllers:
Requirement Analysis: We collaborate closely with customers to define the monitoring objectives and the parameters to be tracked.
Sensor Integration: We select and integrate the appropriate sensors to monitor variables such as temperature, humidity, pressure, flow rates, or any other specific parameters.
Data Acquisition: Our controllers are equipped with robust data acquisition systems to gather data from the sensors in real-time.
User Interface: We design user-friendly interfaces for monitoring controllers, providing users with access to data and control functions.
Alerting and Reporting: Our systems can be configured to send alerts and generate reports based on predefined thresholds or events.
Remote Access: Depending on customer needs, we can enable remote access and control of the monitoring system, allowing users to stay informed and take action from anywhere.
Customization: We offer customization options to tailor the monitoring controller to unique requirements, industries, or applications.
Our commitment to delivering high-quality, customized electronics-based controllers ensures that customers receive solutions that precisely align with their specific needs, whether for IoT applications, monitoring, or other control requirements.
