ABOUT US
Hirush Automation Pte Ltd was established in Singapore as a distinguished Electrical, Control, and Automation Company. In addition to our presence in Singapore, Hirush Automation Pvt Ltd in Sri Lanka also contributes significantly to our success. Our expansion into Sri Lanka has been driven by the same commitment to excellence in Electrical, Control, and Automation services.

The core of our business model is our proficiency in the design, implementation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance of PLC/HMI/SCADA systems and Electrical/Control panels. We specialize in delivering tailored services and have successfully executed numerous projects, solidifying our reputation as leaders in the industry.

MISSION
Our company's mission is to deliver superior quality control and automation systems, paired with exceptional customer service, by relentlessly pursuing innovation and leveraging advanced technology.

VISION
To establish ourselves as one of the leading system integrators and process control automation companies in Singapore and Sri Lanka, committed to delivering exceptional quality services that consistently exceed the expectations of our esteemed customers.

SERVICES

PLC PROGRAMMING
Our expertise lies in proposing a suitable PLC hardware configuration and delivering programming services that align precisely with the control system requirements specified in the contract and the designated control philosophy. We specialize in PLC programming across various brands, including Siemens, Allen-Bradley, and Mitsubishi.

HMI/SCADA DESIGN
We offer SCADA software and communication configuration solutions in consultation with our clients, tailored to meet the contractual requirements and specifications. Our engineering services extend to the development of Human Machine Interface (HMI) graphics, characterized by their robustness, effectiveness, visual appeal, and user-friendliness. Our proficiency spans various brands, encompassing Siemens, Schneider, Allen-Bradley, and Mitsubishi, ensuring comprehensive support for diverse client needs.

ELECTRICAL AND CONTROL PANEL
Control panel design and fabrication constitute integral components of our distinct business offerings. We specialize in crafting high-quality electrical panels, expertly integrated with essential equipment such as drives, starters, and control systems. These panels are carefully outfitted with PLC systems, along with electrical control components, to facilitate precise control and monitoring functions. Moreover, we provide comprehensive drawing and documentation support, ensuring the seamless operation and maintenance of these critical systems.

IOT SOLUTIONS
Our IoT solutions are designed to integrate seamlessly with existing control systems, enabling enhanced data collection, real-time monitoring, and advanced analytics. We provide end-to-end IoT services, including sensor integration, network configuration, data management, and cloud services. Our solutions cater to a wide range of industries, offering improved operational efficiency, predictive maintenance, and data-driven decision-making. By leveraging IoT technologies, we empower our clients to achieve greater visibility and control over their processes.

EMBEDDED CONTROLLER DESIGN
We specialize in the design and development of embedded controllers tailored to meet specific application requirements. Our services encompass the entire development cycle, from initial concept through to final product, including hardware design, firmware development, and system integration. With expertise in various microcontroller platforms and embedded development environments, we deliver robust, efficient, and scalable solutions that ensure optimal performance and reliability for your control systems.
